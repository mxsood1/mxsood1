# Third-party artwork and code

The ASCII juggler is adapted from the animation shared by **shawn. (@zzzzshawn)**, referenced by the profile owner: [original post](https://x.com/zzzzshawn/status/2111545791819194865) and [Designeer reference](https://designeer.xyz/finds/zzzzshawn-2111545791819194865). The original figure, movement, circular brick floor, grass, basket and hat are retained; the balls are replaced with ASCII brand marks. This credit does not apply the shark project's MIT license to the juggler artwork.

Codex, Anthropic and Grok Bot marks identify the respective products and their owners. The Anthropic mark was sourced from [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/), the Grok Bot head from [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot), and the Codex app icon from the existing profile design's reference asset. They are adapted into monochrome terminal characters.

The animated shark silhouettes are adapted from [stackedlol/ascii-sharks](https://github.com/stackedlol/ascii-sharks), commit `a0074c16a106f0abc665fe9712ca445f8f98ad8e`.

The original species geometry and supersampled ASCII rasterizer were adapted for an offline, monochrome, seamless background animation. The sharks are composited behind the existing Masood Corp artwork. No source-project scripts execute in the README.
